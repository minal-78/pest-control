Which Stinging Insect Is Nesting on Your Property?

Different species present unique safety risks and structural nesting habits in Grimsby.

01

Yellowjackets

High Aggression Level

Bright yellow/black striped. Build paper nests underground, inside soffits, or in wall cavities. Highly protective of nest entry points.

02

Paper Wasps

Moderate Aggression

Slender brown with yellow accents. Build open, umbrella-shaped honeycomb nests under roof eaves, porch ceilings, and window frames.

03

Bald-Faced Hornets

Extreme Risk

Black with white facial markings. Construct large, grey teardrop paper nests high in trees, structural gables, or thick shrubs.
